Big Heist — Privacy Notice

Last updated: 2 August 2026

Big Heist is a Twitch Extension panel for an interactive crime-RPG stream game. This notice explains what information the extension accesses and how it is used.

What we access

  • Your Twitch User ID — requested via Twitch's own Identity Link prompt when you open the panel. This is the only personal identifier the extension uses.
  • Your Twitch display name — shown alongside your in-game character so other viewers can see whose progress belongs to whom.

Why we need it

Your Twitch User ID is used purely to link your browser session to your own game data — your character, inventory, skills, and progress in the crime-RPG. Without it, the extension has no way to show you your own character sheet instead of someone else's, or to remember your progress between visits.

What we store

  • Your Twitch User ID and display name.
  • In-game data tied to that ID: your character's mugshot, skills, inventory, in-game currency, and crime/heist history within the stream's game.

This data is stored on the game's own backend server and is used only to run the game shown in the extension panel. It is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose outside making the extension work.

What we don't do

  • We don't access your email address, payment information, or any Twitch account details beyond User ID and display name.
  • We don't use your data for advertising or analytics tracking.
  • We don't share your data with third parties.

YouTube viewers

Viewers watching via a YouTube simulcast can link a standalone version of the panel to their game character using a short one-time code shown privately in the panel and confirmed in YouTube chat. This code is single-use and expires automatically if unclaimed. No YouTube account information beyond a display name you choose to give is stored.

Data removal

If you'd like your data removed, contact the streamer directly.
